# tencent/Hy4-flash

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/tencent/Hy4-flash](https://huggingface.co/tencent/Hy4-flash)

**Description:** Hy4-flash is a multimodal image and text understanding model from tencent. Hy4 flash-base is a pre-trained base model, not an instruction-tuned chat model. The chat and reasoning examples below apply to Hy4 flash and Hy4 flash-FP8.
