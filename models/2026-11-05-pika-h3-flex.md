# Pika-Labs/Pika-H3-Flex

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/Pika-Labs/Pika-H3-Flex](https://huggingface.co/Pika-Labs/Pika-H3-Flex)

**Description:** Pika-H3-Flex is a image-to-video generation model from Pika-Labs. Pika H3 Flex is a post-trained H3 model, designed to give professional creators the flexibility and speed to explore with precision. Optimized for greater control, it generates video with synchronized audio in six sampling steps and supports up to 12 reference inputs, with shot direction through references and prompts.
