# inclusionAI/Ling-3.2-air

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/Ling-3.2-air](https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/Ling-3.2-air)

**Description:** Ling-3.2-air is a machine learning tasks model from inclusionAI. We introduce Ling-3.2-air, an open-weight model that shares the same native multimodal architecture as Ling-3.0-flash-VL. Compared with its predecessor, Ling-3.2-air shows stronger performance in workflow automation and reasoning over professional documents. Its AutomationBench-AA score rises from 15.7% to 61%, while its GDP.pdf all-pass rate increases from 8.4% to 16%.
