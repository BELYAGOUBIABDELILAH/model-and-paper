# moonshotai/Kimi-K3.1-Pro

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/moonshotai/Kimi-K3.1-Pro](https://huggingface.co/moonshotai/Kimi-K3.1-Pro)

**Description:** Kimi-K3.1-Pro is a multimodal image and text understanding model from moonshotai. Kimi K3.1 Pro is an open-weight, natively multimodal agentic model, and our most capable 3T-class model to date. Post-trained from Kimi K3 with large-scale joint reinforcement learning across hundreds of downstream domains, it is the first model in the K3.1 series. With a 1-million-token context window, Kimi K3.1 Pro delivers substantial improvements in coding, 3D creation, financial research, factual accuracy, and other agentic capabilities.
