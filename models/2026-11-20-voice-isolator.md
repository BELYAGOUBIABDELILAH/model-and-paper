# inworld/voice-isolator

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/inworld/voice-isolator](https://huggingface.co/inworld/voice-isolator)

**Description:** voice-isolator is a audio to audio model from inworld. The chart is the denoising board, which ranks every system except those that put more than 10 dB more energy above a band-limited input's band than inside it; those are ranked on restoration.
