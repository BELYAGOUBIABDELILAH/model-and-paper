# Abiray/MiniMax-H3-Image-GGUF

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/Abiray/MiniMax-H3-Image-GGUF](https://huggingface.co/Abiray/MiniMax-H3-Image-GGUF)

**Description:** MiniMax-H3-Image-GGUF is a text-to-image generation model from Abiray. MiniMax-H3-Image is a state-of-the-art 33.1B parameter single-stream Diffusion Transformer (DiT) designed for ultra-high-resolution text-to-image generation and multi-reference image editing (up to 16 MP / 4096 × 4096) in just 6 distilled sampling steps.
