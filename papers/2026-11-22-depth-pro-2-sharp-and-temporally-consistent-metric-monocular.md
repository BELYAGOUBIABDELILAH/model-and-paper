# Depth Pro 2: Sharp and Temporally Consistent Metric Monocular Depth

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26603](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26603)

**Description:** We present Depth Pro 2, an approach to estimating dense metric depth for images and video at full resolution. By analyzing prior methods for monocular depth estimation, we find that they commonly trade off temporal consistency and boundary sharpness: multi-frame methods favor consistency, single-frame methods favor sharpness. To overcome this trade-off, we train a single, scalable network that processes both images and video and predicts depth maps at native resolution.
