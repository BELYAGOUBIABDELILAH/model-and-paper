# Mixture-of-Loops Transformer

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.34923](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.34923)

**Description:** Looped Transformers boost expressivity by reusing shared layers across loops, yet at the cost of efficiency. Pushing their expressivity-efficiency frontier requires rethinking the core mechanism connecting adjacent loops: What should be passed as a Transformer loops? We introduce Mixture-of-Loops Transformer (MoLT), whose Mixture-of-Loops Attention (MoLA) uses keys and values (KV) as memory for selective reuse across depths and loops.
