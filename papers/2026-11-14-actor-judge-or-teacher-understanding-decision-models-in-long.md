# Actor, Judge, or Teacher? Understanding Decision Models in Long-Horizon Agents

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.17167](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.17167)

**Description:** Decision models answer a question with a typed output, such as a choice among options with a probability for each, so one model can choose an agent's actions, evaluate actions already taken, or score every action to teach a policy with reinforcement learning. Prior work has studied them mainly as actors, judging their usefulness by how well they act. We ask whether a decision model that acts poorly can still teach well.
