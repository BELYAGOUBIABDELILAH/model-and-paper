# Learning Heterogeneous Tactile Representation for General Dexterous Manipulation

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.12806](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.12806)

**Description:** Tactile sensing is an indispensable modality for contact-rich dexterous manipulation, a capability that hints at the ultimate frontiers of embodied intelligence. However, commercially available tactile sensors are still at a relatively early stage of development, with each type of sensor offering irreplaceable strengths alongside its own drawbacks. A key challenge is thus to free tactile understanding from sensor-specific physics, so that embodied models can effectively leverage heterogeneous tactile data.
