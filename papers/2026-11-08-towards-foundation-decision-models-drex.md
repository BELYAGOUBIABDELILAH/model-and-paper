# Towards Foundation Decision Models: Drex

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.07854](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.07854)

**Description:** Autoregressive approaches with large language models, such as long chain-of-thought reasoning, have become the default for new AI applications because they reach higher accuracy on a wide range of tasks. In production, however, they give inconsistent answers, require parsing of outputs, and add cost and latency. Decision models address this: given a question and a set of options supplied by the user, they return a probability distribution over the options without autoregressive decoding.
