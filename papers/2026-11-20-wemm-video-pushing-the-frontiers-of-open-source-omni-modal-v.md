# WeMM-Video: Pushing the Frontiers of Open-Source Omni-Modal Video Understanding

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26409](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26409)

**Description:** Video understanding, particularly with accompanying audio, remains a central challenge in multimodal intelligence. In this report, we introduce WeMM-Video, a family of Chinese-English bilingual omni-modal foundation models spanning 2B, 4B, 9B, and 35B-A3B variants.
