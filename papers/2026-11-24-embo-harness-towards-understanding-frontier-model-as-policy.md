# Embo-Harness: Towards Understanding Frontier-Model-as-Policy Harness

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.31201](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.31201)

**Description:** Frontier multimodal models exhibit extraordinary generalization in generating robot actions from observations and instructions, enabling a new paradigm in which they serve directly as policies within closed-loop robotic harnesses. However, the design principles of such harnesses, and the capabilities underlying their strong performance, remain unclear. We present Embo-Harness, a framework for systematically studying frontier-model-as-policy harnesses.
