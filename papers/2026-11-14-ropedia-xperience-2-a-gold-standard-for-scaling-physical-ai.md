# Ropedia Xperience 2: A Gold Standard for Scaling Physical AI Environments and Behaviors

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.17060](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.17060)

**Description:** Large foundation models such as GPT-6 Astra and Claude Fable 5.1 have recently been shown to control robots directly from visual observations. They nevertheless remain brittle on long-horizon, contact-rich tasks in real-world environments. Further progress depends on two complementary resources: demonstrations of human behavior that supervise how tasks are performed, and interactive environments in which policies can be rolled out, evaluated, and improved in closed loop.
