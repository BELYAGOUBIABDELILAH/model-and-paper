# Game2Player: Scaling Gameplay Experience for Visual Agents through Environment-Player Co-Development

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.24154](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.24154)

**Description:** Games are a rich testbed for visual agents, demanding perception, long-horizon planning, and precise control. However, collecting human gameplay is costly, and learning from gameplay videos requires inferring missing actions. Existing datasets also cover a limited range of games, often without explicit tasks or verifiable outcomes.
