# Envisioning Agentic Robotics: A New Era of Robotic Systems

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.18359](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.18359)

**Description:** The release of GPT-6 Astra marks the emergence of a new paradigm in robotics: agentic robotics. Within weeks, frontier models with agentic harnesses matched or exceeded task-specific policies on many embodied benchmarks. Instead of training a dedicated policy for each family of tasks, an agentic robotic system applies a foundation model to open-world embodied tasks.
