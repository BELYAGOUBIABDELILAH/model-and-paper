# Unbundling Multimodal Vision Encoding

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.17334](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.17334)

**Description:** How multimodal language models should encode visual input remains up for debate. Most models use a pretrained vision encoder, while some recent recipes train the encoder from scratch with the language model or remove it entirely. Vision encoders bundle together three design choices: a visual prior from upstream pretraining, dedicated vision capacity, and pre-fusion placement of that capacity (i.e., before the multimodal backbone).
