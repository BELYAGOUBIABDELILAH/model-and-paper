# Lazy Agents Answer Against Their Own Beliefs: Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Beliefs Teaches Them to Act on What They Believe

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.32884](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.32884)

**Description:** AI agents working on long tasks often commit to an answer before they have enough evidence, a behavior called laziness. In our evaluations, they frequently commit even while their stated belief still names several possible answers. An outcome reward at the end of the episode scores only the final answer, so it cannot teach the agent when to commit, because a lucky guess receives the same reward as an answer backed by evidence.
