# Turns Matter: Advancing Streaming Video RL via Turn-Level Credit

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.06728](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.06728)

**Description:** Streaming video understanding requires models to repeatedly decide whether to respond or continue observing. Recently, Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) has been applied to this setting, with existing approaches emphasizing reward design while retaining a single trajectory-level advantage across turns. Such shared credit can reinforce redundant replies after earlier successes and discourage useful follow-ups after earlier failures.
