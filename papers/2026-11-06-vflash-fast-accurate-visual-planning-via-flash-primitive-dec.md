# VFlash: Fast, Accurate Visual Planning via Flash Primitive Decoding

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.08449](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.08449)

**Description:** Models for physical AI tasks, such as robotics and autonomous driving, need not reason solely in language. They can use visual plans--points, bounding boxes, and trajectories--to represent spatial goals and anticipate motion. However, autoregressive vision-language models generate these plans one coordinate token at a time, making richer predictions increasingly costly.
