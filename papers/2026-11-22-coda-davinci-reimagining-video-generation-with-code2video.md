# Coda-DaVinci: Reimagining Video Generation with Code2Video

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26595](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26595)

**Description:** Code2Video uses agent-authored 3D proxies to guide video generation, enabling explicit control over structure and dynamics. However, how to construct proxies and prompts for optimal downstream quality remains underexplored, as do the capabilities and limitations of this paradigm. Through controlled experiments and blinded human evaluation, we study proxy granularity, physics simulation, and prompting.
