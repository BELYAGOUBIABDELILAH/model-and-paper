# Few-Step Streaming Video Generation via Condition Shifting

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.14033](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.14033)

**Description:** Few-step sampling reduces the cost of streaming video generation. Distribution Matching Distillation (DMD) supervises few-step generators with a separate fake-score network, which adds training memory and computation and must track the evolving generator. We extend APEX to streaming video by using condition shifting to estimate the velocity field of the generated distribution within the generator.
