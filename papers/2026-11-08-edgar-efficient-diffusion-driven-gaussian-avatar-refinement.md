# EDGAR: Efficient Diffusion-Driven Gaussian Avatar Refinement via Synthetic Failure

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.07909](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.07909)

**Description:** Recent advances in Gaussian splatting have enabled efficient and photorealistic head avatar reconstruction, yet monocular video-based or image-based methods still suffer from under-constrained geometry and texture, leading to artifacts such as blurring, stretching, and temporal instability. We present EDGAR (Efficient Diffusion-Driven Gaussian Avatar Refinement), a conditional diffusion framework designed to enhance the quality of Gaussian avatars by correcting artifacts while preserving identity fidelity.
