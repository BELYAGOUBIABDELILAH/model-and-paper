# Non-penetrating World Models

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.27963](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.27963)

**Description:** We study a new problem of preventing object penetration in interactive video world models. To understand how existing models handle this, we introduce NoPeBench, a benchmark that evaluates how world models respond to physical obstacles. Our evaluation reveals that none of the open-source models can reliably prevent such failures.
