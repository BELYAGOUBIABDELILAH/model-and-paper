# Posterior Matching Distillation

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26629](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26629)

**Description:** Diffusion distillation accelerates image and video generation by compressing a pretrained teacher's iterative sampling process into a few network evaluations. Distribution-based methods achieve this by matching the student's output distribution to the teacher's. This matching is often driven by reverse-KL objectives, whose mode-seeking bias favors dominant teacher modes at the expense of diversity across samples.
