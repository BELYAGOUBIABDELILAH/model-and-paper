# WorldLeague: Benchmarking Physical Agency

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.30989](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.30989)

**Description:** AI systems can now operate Blender, follow instructions in complex video games, and manipulate objects with robot arms. What range of physical goals can today's AI systems achieve autonomously? Answering this question requires testing systems on tasks that demand different combinations of perception, memory, planning, and action.
