# Real2SimLab: A Unified Platform for Real-to-Simulation Transfer

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.34849](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.34849)

**Description:** Real-to-simulation transfer, which builds simulations from real-world observations, has long been studied in research and industry. Recently, coding agents have become capable of generating simulation programs from video recordings, and several benchmarks have been proposed to evaluate them. However, each individual benchmark defines its own inputs, deliverables, and scoring pipeline.
