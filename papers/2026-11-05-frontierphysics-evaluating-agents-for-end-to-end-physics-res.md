# FrontierPhysics: Evaluating Agents for End-to-End Physics Research

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.06910](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.06910)

**Description:** AI agents are playing an increasingly important role in physics research. However, existing physics benchmarks only evaluate fixed questions, isolated sub-tasks, or paper reproduction with given solutions, and rarely check research quality alongside correctness. We introduce FrontierPhysics, an end-to-end benchmark with 56 tasks made by 42 physicists based on their own research, covering nine domains across theoretical, computational, and experimental physics.
