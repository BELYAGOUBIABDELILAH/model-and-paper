# SO-Arena: Benchmarking the Capability Spectrum and Reliability of System One Models

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.15777](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.15777)

**Description:** System One models promise fast, general-purpose decision-making without lengthy token generation. However, understanding their capability spectrum and decision reliability requires more than aggregate performance on current benchmarks. Existing evaluations often provide limited insight into the breadth of model capabilities and the reliability of their decisions.
