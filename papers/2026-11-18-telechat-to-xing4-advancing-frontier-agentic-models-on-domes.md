# TeleChat to Xing4: Advancing Frontier Agentic Models on Domestic Chips and Frameworks

**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.22115](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.22115)

**Description:** We present Xing 4, a series of large language models that extends the TeleChat lineage through coordinated advances in model architecture and training infrastructure on Ascend. This report describes the design principles and systems engineering behind the series, spanning architecture selection, large-scale pretraining, and reasoning and agentic post-training. We study how sparse activation, attention design, model depth and width, and speculative decoding balance model capacity with computation, memory access,...
